## Understand the observations

- Do not rely on this application for emergency communications, navigation, aviation decisions or other safety-critical purposes.
- A received packet, reported location or successful past traceroute does not guarantee current coverage, delivery or location accuracy. Missing observations do not prove a node is offline.
- Coverage maps summarize observations. Simulations, role comparisons and environmental correlations are exploratory and may be incomplete or wrong. Correlation does not establish causation.
- Weather and aircraft data may be delayed, missing, corrected or sourced indirectly. This project is not an official weather warning or air-traffic service.
- Software tests do not certify radio hardware, field performance, regulatory compliance or native hypervisor compatibility. Refer to the specific documented test results and limits.

## Operator responsibilities

Use only equipment, networks and data sources you are authorized to access. Before enabling transmissions or configuration changes, verify the destination, radio region, settings and applicable operating requirements. Comply with applicable radio rules and third-party service terms; the application does not establish compliance for you.

Back up your configuration and data, protect credentials, control network access, and test upgrades on a separate installation where practical. Collected messages, positions, device identifiers and logs can be sensitive. Review what you collect, retain and share, including exports and screenshots. Optional public forwarding can disclose data outside your private installation; review its settings before enabling it.
