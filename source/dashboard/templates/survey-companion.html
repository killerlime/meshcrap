Use Android 8 or later. Your phone must have access to this collector over trusted HTTPS (for example, through your own Tailscale connection). The app connects directly to an already-paired radio: disconnect the Meshtastic app from that radio first.

Back for another outing?

  1. Keep your saved pairing. Connect Tailscale or your collector’s private network, and disconnect Meshtastic from the travelling radio.
  2. Open Meshcrap Survey and tap Connect radio. Wait for the radio, collector and location to be ready; check the channel.
  3. For a new outing, tap Start survey. If an earlier survey is still active, use Start automatic survey to continue it, or End survey before starting a separate outing.

No coverage-area selection or new pairing code is needed. Pairing survives app restarts, phone reboots and compatible app updates. Generate another code only if pairing was lost or revoked.

2. Pair once with your collector

Open this private HTTPS setup page, use the Unlock button below with your existing dashboard control key. The pairing code is for survey uploads and starting/ending surveys only; it is not a radio admin key.

Checking unlock status…

Remembered browsers can start and stop surveys without re-entering the control key. Admin access, including replacing or revoking a phone pairing, still lasts one hour. Your control key is not saved.

Generating a new code replaces the previous phone pairing. Saved results on the phone remain available for upload after re-pairing.

3. Start your survey

  1. Paste the pairing code into Meshcrap Survey. Allow Bluetooth and location, list paired radios, and connect your chosen local radio.
  2. Confirm the node name, ID and channel shown in the app. Choose the appropriate channel if needed.
  3. Use slot 0, or explicitly choose another channel. Tap Start survey in Android to record a roaming outing across all grids and beyond their boundaries.
  4. The app starts automatic requests after the collector confirms the survey and readiness checks pass. You may send an optional manual test first. A good GPS fix helps select candidates; it does not prove radio reachability.

The app accepts a travelling fix up to 24 hours old with phone accuracy within half a mile; age and reported accuracy remain visible. Nearby candidates show advertised position source, age and precision; GPS positions must be within 12 hours. Last-heard time does not filter or order candidates. Unknown/coarse precision requires a manual test. The default search radius is 25 miles, with 5, 10, 25, 50 and 100-mile choices. This selects candidate nodes; it does not change radio power.

During your outing

Check that the app says Automatic survey running. Starting a new survey from the phone enables requests after collector confirmation and readiness checks. Reconnecting to an existing survey leaves requests paused until you tap Start automatic survey.

  • Pause new requests stops new traceroutes. The current request can finish; GPS and passive reception recording continue while the radio and collector authorization remain available.
  • End survey closes the outing on the collector. Wait for confirmation, then let the queued-record count reach zero before disconnecting when possible.
  • Disconnect and release Bluetooth, or the notification’s Stop, releases the radio for Meshtastic. It does not end the collector’s survey. Already saved records remain queued for a later connection.

If Bluetooth or collector access fails, reconnect as needed and tap Start automatic survey once ready. This is not continuous offline recording: new GPS and passive reception records require a ready radio and a collector authorization renewed within 30 seconds. Previously saved records are retained.

Requests start at least 30 seconds apart, with one outstanding at a time. After 30 seconds without a reply, the app records a timeout; later replies can still be saved while the app retains the request. A timeout or routing failure permits up to three total attempts per destination, with at least 30 seconds after failure before retrying. A successful reply (including a late reply) or the third failure starts an eight-hour cooldown. Manual tests share the same attempt budget. Interrupted or uncertain sends keep their cooldown rather than being retried automatically. This persists across app restarts, outings, channels and travelling radios on this phone. Nodes not yet tested are prioritized, with distance ordering for ties. Last-heard time does not control selection.

GPS shows where you travelled, not proven radio coverage. Replies show reachability, possibly through relays; passive receptions show what the travelling radio heard. Precise map evidence requires phone GPS within two minutes and 100 metres reported accuracy. Approximate records remain saved separately from the collector radio’s measurements.
