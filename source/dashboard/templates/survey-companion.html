Use Android 8 or later. Your phone must have access to this collector over trusted HTTPS (for example, through your own Tailscale connection). The app connects directly to an already-paired radio: disconnect the Meshtastic app from that radio first.

Back for another outing?

Open Meshcrap Survey, leave the saved pairing in place, and connect your radio. Tap Start survey in the Android app; no area selection is needed. You do not need another pairing code.

Your phone keeps its pairing encrypted through app restarts, phone reboots, and app updates. Pair again only after clearing app data, reinstalling, or replacing/revoking its access.

2. Pair once with your collector

Open this private HTTPS setup page, use the Unlock button below with your existing dashboard control key. The pairing code is for survey uploads and starting/ending surveys only; it is not a radio admin key.

Checking unlock status…

Remembered browsers can start and stop surveys without re-entering the control key. Admin access, including replacing or revoking a phone pairing, still lasts one hour. Your control key is not saved.

Generating a new code replaces the previous phone pairing. Saved results on the phone remain available for upload after re-pairing.

3. Start your survey

  1. Paste the pairing code into Meshcrap Survey. Allow Bluetooth and location, list paired radios, and connect your chosen local radio.
  2. Confirm the node name, ID and channel shown in the app. Choose the appropriate channel if needed.
  3. Use slot 0, or explicitly choose another channel. Tap Start survey in Android to record a roaming outing across all grids and beyond their boundaries.
  4. The app starts automatic requests after the collector confirms the survey and readiness checks pass. You may send an optional manual test first. A good GPS fix helps select candidates; it does not prove radio reachability.

The app accepts a travelling fix up to one hour old with phone accuracy within half a mile; age and reported accuracy remain visible. Nearby candidates show advertised position source, age and precision; GPS positions must be within five minutes and last-heard time within 15 minutes. Unknown/coarse precision requires a manual test. The default search radius is 25 miles, with 5, 10, 25, 50 and 100-mile choices. This selects candidate nodes; it does not change radio power.

During your outing

One request at a time, at least 30 seconds apart, with up to 30 seconds for a response. Candidates can be tested again after half a mile of movement with precise GPS (at least 60 seconds since the previous test), or after five minutes. Requests pause if Bluetooth or the collector connection is lost. A lost connection may take up to 30 seconds to expire.

Use End survey in the app to close the outing. Use the notification’s Pause or Stop controls to pause requests or release Bluetooth. Stop releases Bluetooth so you can reconnect Meshtastic. Saved results upload on the next connection; no uncertain request is automatically resent after a restart.

Phone GPS records your path. It does not automatically turn a cell into proven RF coverage. Phone traceroutes and passive receptions are stored separately from measurements heard by Receiver. Precise map evidence requires phone GPS within two minutes and 100 metres reported accuracy; approximate records remain saved.
