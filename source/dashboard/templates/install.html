Meshcrap on your iPhone

CRAP = CuriousityReportingAndPossibilties
Explore your mesh, report observations, and investigate possibilities.

Your dashboard and controls in a Home Screen app. No App Store account or Mac required.

  1. Connect to the network or Tailscale that reaches your collector.
  2. Open this dashboard’s private HTTPS address in Safari.
  3. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen. If offered, enable Open as Web App, then tap Add.
  4. Open Meshcrap from its new icon. Unlock controls once and select Remember on your own device.

The app uses the same permissions as the website. Bluetooth surveys are not included in this version. Keep the Android companion for those.

A connection to the collector is required. Offline, no commands are queued or sent later. Messages, telemetry, and credentials are not stored in the offline cache. The installed app may require its own initial sign-in.
